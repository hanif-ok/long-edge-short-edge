Flip on long edge or short edge? See how your printout comes out

Double-sided printing, several pages per sheet, booklets and posters often come out with pages upside down or in the wrong order. Set the options below the way you would in Adobe Reader's Print window. The preview underneath shows the paper that comes out of the printer. Pick it up with your mouse or finger and turn it over to see which page ends up where, and which way up.

Long edge or short edge: which one do I need?

Flip on long edge and Flip on short edge name an edge of the paper, not a direction. The printer prints the back so that it reads the right way up after you turn the sheet over that edge. Which way that is depends on how you hold the sheet:

  • Portrait (tall) sheet: the long edges are the left and right sides, so flipping on the long edge means turning it sideways, like a book.
  • Landscape (wide) sheet: the long edges are the top and bottom, so flipping on the long edge means turning it top over bottom, like a wall calendar.

Turn a sheet over the other edge and its back comes out exactly upside down.

Why is the back of my landscape printout upside down?

On a landscape sheet the long edges are the top and bottom, so Flip on long edge prints the back for turning the sheet top over bottom. Turn it sideways instead and the back is upside down. Choose Flip on short edge if people will turn the pages sideways. To see it for yourself, set Page shape to Landscape above, tick Print on both sides of paper, then drag the top sheet left and compare with dragging it up.

Which flip setting do I need for 2 pages per sheet?

Two pages per sheet puts two portrait pages side by side on a landscape sheet, so choose Flip on short edge if the sheets will be turned sideways. The same usually applies to 6 pages per sheet. With 4, 9 or 16 pages per sheet the sheet usually stays portrait, so Flip on long edge is right.

How do I print a booklet in Adobe Reader?

In the Print window, choose Booklet under Page Sizing & Handling, set Booklet subset to Both sides and Binding to Left (or Right for right-to-left languages). Reader puts the pages in folding order and adds blank pages to make a multiple of four. Fold the stack in half and staple along the fold. Fold into a booklet in the preview shows the result. If the backs come out upside down, set your printer's own two-sided setting to flip on the short edge.

What do Size, Poster, Multiple and Booklet do?

  • Size: one page per sheet, scaled with Fit, Actual size, Shrink oversized pages or a custom percentage.
  • Poster: one large page split across several sheets that you tape together, with optional overlap, cut marks and labels.
  • Multiple: several pages on each sheet (2, 4, 6, 9, 16 or your own grid), in the page order you pick.
  • Booklet: pages arranged so the printed sheets can be folded into a booklet.

About this simulation

  • Each sheet is drawn the way you would hold it to read the front. Your printer may feed the paper in a different direction.
  • Your printer driver (the Properties button in Reader) has its own two-sided setting. If it disagrees with Reader's, the driver often wins.
  • Pages that Auto portrait/landscape or Auto-rotate turn sideways are drawn turned a quarter turn counterclockwise. Some printers turn them the other way.
  • For booklets, Reader picks the flip edge itself, so the two-sided setting is grayed out. Posters are shown printed on one side only.
  • The printer is assumed to leave a 1/4 inch (6.35 mm) blank border on every side.
  • Your own PDF is drawn by PDF.js (Mozilla, Apache License 2.0). Each page gets a small tag with its number and an arrow pointing to its top.
